# XNU patch series

Applied in order by CI. `sweet` patches are 0001-0079;
santoni/lavender/whyred patches start at 0080. Each row is one patch and
the reason it exists. Tests for every row live in `tests/run_tests.sh`.

| 0220 | Clear the F01 sleep bits and the F12 `ctrl20` wakeup-gesture byte (the vendor's `synaptics_rmi4_f12_wg(false)`) before writing `ctrl28`, because `ctrl28` alone leaves a part in LPWG with its 2D block inactive. | 0223 | Lower the bit-bang line wait from 2 ms to 500 us, override it with `sweetouchscl=<us>` (clamped to 10..20000), and add `sweetouchfast=1`, which skips the two 0x200 stream reads and uses this unit's known F01 (`0x2f/0x26/0x0d/0x04`) and F12 (`0x4c/0x13/0x06`) bases. | 0224 | Make `whyred_touch_power_cycle()` distinguish `1` (rail on after the toggle), `0` (`sweetouchpower` absent) and `-1` (arbiter refused the APID). | 0225 | Tie the DSX to the display: after the late probe, `whyred_touch_display_power()` pauses the HID poll, waits for an in-flight read and writes F01 `SENSOR_SLEEP` on display off; on display on it writes `NORMAL_OPERATION | NO_SLEEP_ON` (preserving `CONFIGURED`), programs F12 again and resumes the poll. | 0226 | Bound `sweetfb_stayon=1` to the boot window: display-off is ignored only before the first userspace present (`sweet_fb_present_logged == 0`), so the power button and auto-lock can take effect afterwards. | 0227 | Send DSX writes through the QUP: `dsx_write_f()` refused every write once `sweeti2c=1` made BLSP1 QUP1 the bus master, even though `sweet_i2c_xfer()` ends a write-only transfer with a stop and accepts four bytes. The HID poll is split into three-record chunks on QUP because the FIFO is at least 32 bytes while a ten-finger read is 80 bytes plus two tag bytes; the bit-bang path still reads the whole block. | 0228 | Drop presents while `fPowerOff`: the 2026-11-22 ring keeps presenting after display-off (744 `present` lines after its second request), and whyred has no DSI panel-off, so a frame would overwrite the software fade and relight the panel. `AppleCLCD::present()` samples `fPowerOff` under `fLock` at entry and returns before `directPresent()` or the copy path. Display-on already marks `fDirty`, so the vsync timer repaints the latest pending swap. Not booted |
